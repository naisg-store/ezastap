Details & What to Expect

Included in the price above (What you don't get billed for) +
  • Placing your logo on one of our standard keychain shapes.
  • One digital proof, plus one round of minor revisions.
  • Programming the NFC chip with your one shared link.
  • Setup is free at 25+ pieces (or completely waived under local prepped sample promos).
Worth asking about later (Things that can add cost) +
  • Logo isn't vector art yet, or needs complex redrawing/cleanup ($75–$125 artwork prep).
  • A fully custom physical outline/shape instead of a standard one.
  • Personalized names or numbers printed on each keychain ($2.00/unit).
  • A different unique link on every single keychain, instead of one shared link ($2.00/unit).
What decides whether artwork setup is extra? +

If you have an original vector file (.SVG, .AI, .EPS, or vector .PDF) or if we already vectorized your artwork for a sample print, setup is 100% free! If you only have a low-res PNG/JPG, screenshot, or artwork that needs to be recreated from scratch, standard setup fees apply for small batches.
